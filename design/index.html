// banner_kit

WordCamp Bengaluru 2026 banners.

Every banner in the kit, previewed live. Open one, hit Download PNG, and you get a pixel-exact export at its native size. Serve this folder over HTTP (./serve.sh) rather than opening it off the disk, or the export will trip over canvas security.

// early_bird

Tickets Are Live

The Early Bird launch. The canvas is the ticket: full bleed, perforated down the right, punched at both ends of the tear, the engraving clipped dead at the perforation the way a real ticket's artwork stops there. No object in the corner, because the poster is the object. Three sizes, not two — the 4:5 exists because Instagram's profile grid crops a square poster to pieces.

Tickets Are Live Square1080 × 1080 LinkedIn, X and WhatsApp. Fine as a post everywhere; do not use it as the Instagram feed image, because the profile grid takes 137px off each side of a square and cuts the headline's first letter off.
